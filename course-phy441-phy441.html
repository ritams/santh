PHY 441 / 6414 : Quantum Information

This is a first course on the fundamentals of quantum information and quantum computation. This course is suitable for advanced undergraduates and PhD students who have already taken a first course in quantum mechanics.
This website will have all the updates for this course.

This course will cover the following topics (download PDF file) :

  • Basic ideas of theory of computation, states and operators, no-cloning theorem
  • Qubits, quantum gates and circuits, measurements.
  • Quantum teleportation, Superdense coding, Deutsch Jozsa algorithm.
  • Schmidt decomposition, Quantum entanglements and their measures.
  • Bell's inequality.
  • Quantum fourier transform, search and prime factorisation.
  • Physical implementations and practical session with quantum computers.
  • Quantum error correction or quantum machine learning (if time permits)
