PHY 102 : Waves and Matter

This is the first undergraduate course in wave phenomena and properties of matter.

This course will cover the following topics :

  • Oscillations and waves in one and many dimensions.
  • Damped and driven oscillations. Resonances.
  • Oscillations of continuous string and Fourier analysis.
  • Travelling waves, pulses and wave packets,
    phase and group velocity, reflection, refraction and transmission.
  • Sound waves, speed of sound.
  • Properties of deformable media, Hooke's law, stress and strain.
  • Torsion and bending of rods, deflections of rods.
